調式練習 · 一音變身MODES PRACTICE · ONE-NOTE CHANGE

開口唱練習 · 只動一個音,換一個調式 · Move one note, change the mode

這是什麼?WHAT IS THIS

全曲 8 組、92 小節。每一組先唱來源音階,再只改一個音(金色),最後用新調式自己的首調唱名再唱一次。例如大調的 mi 降半音變成 me,音階就變成旋律小調;下一行改用旋律小調的首調唱名 la ti do re mi fi si la 再唱。

唱名採首調(母大調)唱法:每個調式都用它的母大調唱名,例如 Dorian 唱 re mi fa sol la ti do re,也就是 2 3 4 5 6 7 1 2。變化的音唱 me、te、fi、di、si。數字上方的點表示高八度,右側的 ♭ ♯ 表示降半音、升半音。

There are 8 groups and 92 bars. Each group first sings a source scale, then the same scale with exactly one note changed (in gold), then the new mode once more in its own parent-major syllables. For example, lowering mi of the major scale gives me, and the scale becomes melodic minor; the next row re-sings it as la ti do re mi fi si la.

The solfege is parent-major (movable-do): every mode uses the syllables of its parent major scale, so Dorian is re mi fa sol la ti do re = 2 3 4 5 6 7 1 2. Changed notes are sung me, te, fi, di and si. A dot above a number means the octave above; ♭ and ♯ mean a semitone lower and higher.

怎麼練HOW TO PRACTISE

先聽一遍,第二遍開始跟著唱。注意金色那個唱名:來源音階唱到它的位置時,下一行就換成變化唱名,聽出這一個音的差別,就聽出調式的差別。最後一行用新調式自己的唱名唱,對比會很明顯。8 組唱完,換一個起始音再來。

Listen once, then sing along. Watch the gold syllable: where the source scale has the old note, the next row has the altered one. Hearing that one note is hearing the mode change. The last row re-sings the new mode in its own syllables, and the contrast is obvious. After all 8 groups, pick a new starting note and go again.
